# Sealed Bidding (FAR Part 14)

**One-line:** Price-only competition by rigid ritual: IFB → public bid
opening → award to the lowest responsive, responsible bidder — with the
responsiveness and bid-mistake rules doing most of the litigating.

*Compiled from 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook ch. 8 (whole
chapter).*

## When sealed bidding is mandatory (the *Racal* factors)
Agencies MUST use sealed bids when all four hold: time permits; award
will be on price and price-related factors only; no discussions needed;
more than one bid reasonably expected (41 U.S.C. § 3301(b)). Choosing
negotiation instead requires a reasonable explanation of which factor
fails — adding non-price factors like past performance usually suffices
(*Ceres Envtl.*). Contract types: FFP or FP-EPA only.

## Process notes
- **Notice**: posting on SAM.gov (the GPE) = constructive notice to
  everyone, incumbents included; after posting, obtaining the
  solicitation is the bidder's problem (*Optelec*). GAO asks who had
  the "last clear opportunity" to avoid the miss.
- **Submission**: bids must reach the designated office by the exact
  bid-opening time; mailroom receipt doesn't count; the bid-opening
  officer's declared time controls unless unreasonable (30 seconds late
  is late). Only transmission methods the IFB authorizes are valid.
  Late-opening postponement only for mail/comms delays or events
  interrupting normal Government processes.
- **Firm bid rule**: after opening, bids are irrevocable for the stated
  acceptance period; offering a shorter period than the required
  minimum = nonresponsive (period affects price, can't be waived).
- Amendments: material ones (any real effect on price/quantity/quality/
  delivery or legal obligations) must be acknowledged in writing —
  failure = nonresponsive.

## Responsiveness — determined at bid opening, from the face of the bid
A bid must unequivocally offer to perform per the IFB. Classic
killers: qualified price (taxes "if applicable"), quantity/quality/
delivery deviations, unsigned bid (unless intent to be bound shown
elsewhere), unacknowledged material amendment, conditional terms,
missing/defective **bid guarantee** (penal sum is material; the bond
must be enforceable on its face at opening, *All Seasons*, 55 Fed. Cl.
175). Nonresponsive bids can never be cured after opening — even to
save money (*MIBO*).

**Minor informalities** (RFO FAR 14.306-7) may be cured or waived: copy
counts, missing employee data, missing signature with other evidence of
intent, immaterial amendment acknowledgments, small-business
certification errors, missing line-item prices on all-or-none awards.
Test: divisible, de minimis, and no effect on competitive standing.

## Bid mistakes
- **Before award**: CO must examine bids and **verify** any suspected
  mistake (big price gaps = constructive notice); inadequate
  verification lets the contractor later seek rescission/adjustment.
  - Apparent clerical errors (decimal points, unit mix-ups): CO corrects
    after verification.
  - Other mistakes: correction requires **clear and convincing
    evidence** of both the mistake and the intended bid (worksheets,
    sub quotes); if correction would **displace a lower bidder**, the
    evidence must be obvious from the bid documents themselves and the
    agency head must approve.
  - Judgment errors (misread specs, bad estimates) are never
    correctable; withdrawal is the fallback where the mistake is proven
    but the intended bid isn't.
- **After award**: CDA territory (GAO won't touch it). Reformation/
  rescission possible where the mistake was mutual, or unilateral but
  so apparent the CO was on notice (price 62% over estimate did it,
  *Government Micro Resources*); elements for unilateral-mistake
  reformation include clear clerical error, Government knowledge, and
  no verification request.

## Cancellation & award
- Pre-opening: cancel for almost any public-interest reason. Post-
  opening: **compelling reason required** (exposed prices make it "a
  serious matter") — statutory violation, funds insufficient,
  requirement gone, defective specs, all prices unreasonable, missed
  set-aside, missing wage determination.
- Award: reasonable promptness, lowest responsive responsible bid,
  price and price-related factors only; options added to evaluation
  unless it wouldn't benefit the Government; reject **materially
  unbalanced** bids posing risk; verify suspiciously low prices (below-
  cost bids are legal — the check is responsibility). Mailbox rule:
  award effective on dispatch of written notice.

## Gotchas / lessons learned
- Sealed bidding forgives nothing at the margins: seconds late is late,
  a short acceptance period is fatal, and the bid bond must be perfect
  at opening.
- For the Government, the verification duty is the exposure point —
  a waved-through low bid with a suspected mistake invites rescission.
