# Litigation at the Court of Federal Claims (Protests & CDA)

**One-line:** What changes when a protest or contract dispute moves from
the agency/GAO into the COFC — DOJ takes over, review is on the
administrative record (protests) or de novo (CDA), and injunctions
replace the automatic CICA stay.

*Compiled from 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook ch. 15B (whole
chapter). Companion to [Bid Protests](bid-protests.md) (agency/GAO) and
the [Contract Disputes Act](contract-disputes-act.md) page.*

## Court basics
- Article I court, 16 active judges, 15-year terms; no juries; DC-based
  but sits nationwide. Appeals go to the Federal Circuit.
- **DOJ (National Courts Section) represents the United States** — once
  a case is filed, the agency and the CO lose authority to decide or
  settle claims on the same operative facts (*Sharman*, 2 F.3d 1564).
- Tucker Act: 6-year statute of limitations, **jurisdictional** (*John
  R. Sand & Gravel*, 552 U.S. 130 — no equitable tolling). Contract
  claims require authority, privity (narrow third-party-beneficiary
  exception), and implied-in-fact (never implied-in-law) contracts.
  NAFIs are covered post-*Slattery* (635 F.3d 1298).

## Bid protests at COFC (28 U.S.C. § 1491(b))
- Jurisdiction over anything "in connection with a procurement" —
  broadly construed (*Distributed Solutions*, 539 F.3d 1340), including
  **CICA stay overrides** (*RAMCOR*, 185 F.3d 1286). But NOT task/
  delivery orders (FASA bar; *SRA Int'l*, 766 F.3d 1409 — the >$10M/$35M
  exceptions run to GAO only), and not violations of non-procurement
  statutes.
- **Standing**: "interested party" = actual/prospective bidder with
  direct economic interest (*Percipient.ai* en banc, 153 F.4th 1226
  (Fed. Cir. 2025) — one definition for all § 1491(b) protests);
  post-*CACI* (67 F.4th 1145) it's statutory standing, not
  jurisdictional. Post-award prejudice = substantial chance of award;
  some pre-award protests need only non-trivial competitive injury
  (*Weeks Marine*).
- **Blue & Gold waiver**: fail to challenge a patent solicitation defect
  before close of bidding and you've waived it in court (492 F.3d 1308;
  extended by *COMINT* and *Inserso*; nonjurisdictional per *M.R.
  Pittman*, 68 F.4th 1275).
- **Review**: APA arbitrary-and-capricious on the **administrative
  record** — no discovery free-for-all, supplementation only when the
  record precludes effective judicial review (*Axiom*, 564 F.3d 1379).
  Presumption of regularity; agency following a rational GAO
  recommendation is protected (*Honeywell*, 870 F.2d 644).
- **No automatic stay** at COFC — the protester needs a TRO/PI under the
  four-factor test (merits, irreparable harm, balance of harms, public
  interest; national defense gets statutory "due regard," 28 U.S.C.
  § 1491(b)(3)). Monetary relief capped at bid & proposal costs; EAJA
  fees possible.
- Practical flow: 24-hour pre-filing notice to DOJ → status conference
  within ~a day (voluntary stay discussed) → AR filed → briefing.
  Agencies should start assembling the Appendix C administrative record
  the moment a COFC protest looks likely, and call DOJ **before**
  finalizing any stay-override D&F.

## CDA cases at COFC (preview — details on the CDA page)
- Prerequisites: proper claim to the CO + final decision (or deemed
  denial). Review is **de novo** — the CO's findings bind no one, and
  the Government may defend on grounds the CO never knew (*Empire
  Energy*, 362 F.3d 1343).
- Clocks: claim to CO within 6 years of accrual (not jurisdictional,
  *Sikorsky*, 773 F.3d 1315); complaint within **12 months** of the
  final decision. A deemed-denied claim never starts the 12-month
  clock. Reconsideration that actually occurs suspends finality.
- **Election doctrine**: choosing COFC vs. the board is binding once
  made (unless the chosen forum lacked jurisdiction). Board decisions
  don't bind the COFC.
- Subcontractors: no privity, no CDA suit (third-party beneficiaries may
  have a Tucker Act path, *FloorPro*). Sureties: only after a takeover
  agreement (*Guarantee Co. v. Ikhana*).

## Gotchas / lessons learned
- COFC protests have no automatic stay — if the agency won't stay
  voluntarily, the fight is won or lost at the TRO/PI stage.
- *Blue & Gold* makes "protest the solicitation now or never" the rule
  in court, mirroring GAO's timeliness trap.
- Contemporaneous documentation is again decisive: record review means
  the agency's file IS the case.
