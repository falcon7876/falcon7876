# Contract Types

**One-line:** How federal contracts are categorized — by structure (base +
options, indefinite-delivery, letter contracts) and by price arrangement
(fixed-price → cost-reimbursement spectrum) — and how each allocates cost
risk between contractor and Government.

*Compiled from 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook ch. 7 (whole chapter).
Note: the 2026 edition cites the FAR Overhaul ("RFO FAR"); FAR part/subpart
numbers below follow the deskbook's RFO citations.*

## The two axes (ch. 7 at 7-1)

1. **Price arrangement** — who bears cost risk: fixed-price (contractor)
   ↔ cost-reimbursement (Government), with incentive contracts between.
2. **Structure** — how quantity/duration is arranged: base + option
   periods, indefinite-delivery contracts, T&M/LH, letter contracts.

Label disputes are resolved by substance, not the cover page: *Crown
Laundry & Dry Cleaners, Inc. v. United States*, 29 Fed. Cl. 506 (1993).

## Risk spectrum (ch. 7 fig. 10 at 7-49)

Most Government risk → most contractor risk:
`CPFF-LOE → T&M → CPFF → CPAF → CPIF → cost/no-fee → cost-sharing → FPI → FP-EPA → FFP`

## Structure types

### Base + options (RFO FAR 17.2) — ch. 7 at 7-2 to 7-6
- Option = unilateral Government right (FAR 2.201). Typical structure:
  1-yr base + four 1-yr options; service contracts capped at 5 years
  under 41 U.S.C. 6707(d).
- **Exercise requirements** (RFO FAR 17.204-1(b)): written notice within
  the contract's window; funds available; need continues; option was
  synopsized/competed; contractor not excluded in SAM; performance
  acceptable; price still fair and reasonable. DoD adds SAM + CMMC/SPRS
  verification (RFO DFARS 217.204.170).
- **Strict mechanics**: late or out-of-sequence exercise invalidates the
  option (*Lockheed Martin Corp. v. Walker*, 149 F.3d 1377 (Fed. Cir.
  1998); *The Boeing Co.*, ASBCA 37579; *White Sands Constr.*, ASBCA
  51875). Adding new terms at exercise violates CICA (*4737 Connor Co.*,
  65 F. App'x 274 (Fed. Cir. 2003); *Varo, Inc.*, ASBCA 47945).
- Improperly exercised + contractor performs anyway → possible equitable
  adjustment as constructive change (*Lockheed Martin IR Imaging*, 108
  F.3d 319 (1997)).
- Extensions **beyond scope** = new procurement subject to CICA
  ("bridge contracts"); poor planning is no excuse (41 U.S.C.
  § 3304(e)(5)(A)(i)). Distinguish extensions under 52.217-8/-9 that
  were part of the original competition — see
  [FAR 52.217-9](far-52-217-9-option-to-extend.md).
- Deciding **not** to exercise is contract administration — generally
  not protestable (*Young-Robinson Assoc.*, B-242229).

### Indefinite-delivery contracts (RFO FAR 16.5) — ch. 7 at 7-7 to 7-18
Three types (RFO FAR 16.501-2(a)):
- **Definite-quantity/indefinite-delivery** (16.502): quantity and price
  fixed; delivery orders set time/place.
- **Requirements** (16.503): Government promises ALL its requirements to
  one contractor. Buying elsewhere (or in-house) = breach (*Datalect*,
  56 Fed. Cl. 178 (2003); *Torncello*, 681 F.2d 756 (Ct. Cl. 1982) —
  narrow holding per *Salsbury*, 905 F.2d 1518). Estimates must be
  realistic, based on reasonably available info (*Womack*, 389 F.2d 793;
  GAO sustains protests on flawed estimates, *Beldon Roofing*,
  B-277651); negligent estimates → reliance damages, not lost profits
  (*Rumsfeld v. Applied Cos.*, 325 F.3d 1329 (Fed. Cir. 2003)).
  Good-faith variation of requirements allowed (*Technical Assistance
  Int'l*, 150 F.3d 1369). >$150M single-award needs special
  determination.
- **ID/IQ** (16.504): Government must order a stated, **non-nominal
  minimum** (else illusory — *Satellite Servs.*, B-280945); contractor
  entitled only to the minimum (*Travel Centre v. Barram*, 236 F.3d 1316
  (Fed. Cir. 2001)). Can't retroactively T4C to dodge an unordered
  minimum (*Maxima Corp.*, 847 F.2d 1549 (Fed. Cir. 1988)). Policy
  preference for **multiple award** (16.504-3); on-/off-ramps allowed
  (16.504-4).
- **Fair opportunity** for orders under multiple-award vehicles (RFO FAR
  16.507): tiers at micro-purchase, SAT, $7.5M, with DoD debriefs
  required ≥$10M orders. Task/delivery-order **protests barred** except
  (1) order exceeds scope/period/max value of the parent, or (2) DoD
  orders >$35M (10 U.S.C. § 3406(f)) / civilian >$10M (41 U.S.C.
  § 4106(f)); plus down-selections and small-business set-aside
  transfers (*LBM, Inc.*, B-290682). Agency must designate a task-order
  ombudsman (16.507-2(b)).

### Letter contracts / UCAs (RFO FAR 16.603) — ch. 7 at 7-18
Binding go-ahead when work must start immediately; HCA approval, NTE
price required. Definitize by the earlier of 180 days or 50% of ceiling
obligated (10 U.S.C. § 3372); Government liability capped at 50% of
estimated cost pre-definitization.

## Price types

### Fixed-price family (RFO FAR 16.2) — ch. 7 at 7-20 to 7-25
- **FFP** (16.202): price never adjusts for contractor's cost
  experience; contractor bears overruns (*Chevron U.S.A.*, ASBCA 32323),
  keeps underruns. Required for sealed bids and (with FP-EPA) for
  commercial products/services (16.201).
- **FP-EPA** (16.203): price adjusts on specified triggers — established
  prices, actual labor/material costs (increases capped at 10%,
  52.216-4), or cost indexes (DFARS guidance). Courts reform defective
  EPA clauses (*Beta Sys.*, 838 F.2d 1179 (Fed. Cir. 1988)); missing the
  contractual request window waives the adjustment (*Bataco*, 29 Fed.
  Cl. 318).
- **FP with prospective redetermination** (16.204) and **fixed-ceiling
  retroactive redetermination** (16.205); **FFUP** — fixed unit price,
  quantity open except minimum/ceiling.

### Cost-reimbursement family (RFO FAR 16.3) — ch. 7 at 7-25 to 7-31
- Use only when requirements/costs are too uncertain for fixed pricing
  (16.301-2(a)); needs approved acquisition plan, adequate contractor
  **cost accounting system** (*CrystaComm*, ASBCA 37177), COR
  designated; **barred for commercial products/services**.
- Contractor owes only **best efforts** up to ceiling (*General
  Dynamics*, 671 F.2d 474 (Ct. Cl. 1982)); allowable costs =
  reasonable + allocable + accounted + not disallowed (RFO FAR 31.201-2).
- **CPPC is statutorily prohibited** (10 U.S.C. § 3322(a)); test from
  *Urban Data Sys.*, 699 F.2d 1147 (Fed. Cir. 1983): predetermined %
  rate applied to actual costs, entitlement uncertain at award and
  rising with costs.
- Variants: **cost/no-fee** (16.302, R&D with nonprofits),
  **cost-sharing** (16.303), **CPFF** (16.304; completion vs. term
  form; statutory fee caps).

### T&M / Labor-hour (RFO FAR 16.6) — ch. 7 at 7-32
Own category, neither FP nor cost. Only when extent/duration of work
can't be estimated; requires D&F that nothing else suits, price ceiling,
HCA approval, and Government surveillance (no cost-control incentive —
*CACI v. GSA*, GSBCA 15588). LH = T&M minus materials.

### Incentive contracts (RFO FAR 16.4) — ch. 7 at 7-33 to 7-47
- D&F signed by HCA required for all incentive/award-fee contracts
  (16.401-2). Every incentive contract must include a **cost incentive**
  (16.403-1(a)).
- **Award fee** (16.402): for subjectively-measured performance;
  award-fee plan + board required; adjectival ratings cap the earnable
  pool (Excellent 91–100% … Satisfactory ≤50%, Unsatisfactory 0%). DoD:
  ≥40% of pool reserved for final evaluation; interim payments outside
  period-end evaluations prohibited (DFARS 216.402-4).
- **FPI** (16.404): target cost + target profit + share ratio + price
  ceiling (no profit ceiling/floor). Ceiling typically 115–135% of
  target cost; if ~150% needed, use CPIF instead (Cibinic & Nash).
  Firm-target vs. successive-targets forms.
- **CPIF** (16.405): target cost/fee, min/max fee, share formula. If
  contractor meets the stated incentive criteria, Government must pay
  max fee despite minor quibbles (*North American Rockwell*, ASBCA
  14329).

## Selecting the type (RFO FAR 16.102/16.103) — ch. 7 at 7-48
Negotiable, sound-judgment call; CO's reasonable discretion is protest-
proof (*Diversified Tech.*, B-282497). Must document selection rationale
in the plan/file (16.103, with narrow exceptions). Avoid staying in
cost-type/T&M once pricing experience supports firmer pricing (16.102(c)).

## Gotchas / lessons learned
- Check the fill-ins and mechanics before every option exercise — the
  case law invalidates options for foot-faults (timing, sequence, new
  terms).
- On ID/IQs, the guaranteed minimum is the whole enforceable deal;
  everything else is discretionary orders.
- "The contract type on page 1" doesn't control — structure and clauses
  do.
