# Negotiated Procurements & Source Selection (FAR Part 15)

**One-line:** The best-value world: RFPs with disclosed evaluation
factors, evaluations that must match those factors exactly, optional but
regulated negotiations, a documented tradeoff decision — and debriefings
whose clocks drive protests.

*Compiled from 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook ch. 9 (whole
chapter). Note: the 2025 FAR Overhaul renamed "discussions" to
"negotiations" and added the HTR-FRP approach; older case law uses the
old vocabulary.*

## Choosing Part 15 and building the RFP
- Negotiation is required whenever any *Racal* sealed-bidding factor
  fails (usually: non-price factors matter or discussions may be
  needed); the CO's reasonable judgment controls (*Weeks Marine*, 575
  F.3d 1352). See [Sealed Bidding](sealed-bidding.md) comparison.
- RFP core: Section C (SOW/PWS), L (instructions — offerors ignore page
  limits/formatting at their peril, *Mathews*), M (evaluation criteria).
  Draft C/L/M together and keep L and M consistent.
- Solicitations must disclose all significant factors/subfactors and
  their **relative importance**, including whether non-price factors
  combined are more/less/equal to cost-price (10 U.S.C. § 3206(c)).
  Silence = factors presumed equal. Cost/price and quality must ALWAYS
  be evaluated; past performance is required (RFO FAR 15.104(b)(3);
  DoD thresholds in DFARS 215.104-70) unless the CO documents why not.
- Proposal time: ≥30 days over the SAT (45 for R&D). Late-proposal rule
  mirrors "late is late" with narrow exceptions (electronic receipt by
  5 pm the prior working day; Government control; only proposal). No
  firm-bid rule — offerors may withdraw anytime pre-award.

## Source selection approaches (the best-value continuum, RFO FAR 15.103)
- **Tradeoff**: pay more for merit; the rationale must be documented —
  mechanical point-score comparisons get sustained protests (*Shumaker
  Trucking*; *C&B Constr.*).
- **LPTA**: lowest price among technically acceptable; pass/fail
  quality; no extra credit. Congress disfavors it — avoid for
  knowledge-based services, IT/cyber, PPE, safety-critical items (2017-
  2019 NDAAs; DFARS 215.103-270). Unacceptable small-business past
  performance under LPTA → SBA COC referral.
- **HTR-FRP** (new): rank on technical merit only, then check the top
  proposal's price for fair-and-reasonableness; iterate down. No
  tradeoffs allowed; must be declared in advance.
- **Phased**: staged downselects declared in the RFP.

## Evaluation discipline (where protests are won)
- Evaluate on the stated criteria — nothing else. Unstated criteria,
  unstated minimums, or ignoring stated tests are "generally fatal"
  (*Y&K Maint.*; *Ashbury* — promised testing skipped). Considerations
  *reasonably encompassed* by stated factors are fine; heavily weighted
  hidden subfactors are not (*Lloyd H. Kessler*).
- Treat offerors equally (*Brican* — same subcontractor, unequal
  credit); document strengths/weaknesses/risks — ratings are guides,
  not decisions; the SSA must "look behind the ratings."
- **Price analysis** (fixed-price): reasonableness = too high, never
  too low; below-cost FFP offers are legal. **Price realism** is
  optional and only if the RFP says so; prices may not be adjusted.
- **Cost realism** (cost-type): mandatory — evaluate *probable* cost,
  adjust proposed costs, analyze each offeror individually, document it
  (*KPMG*).
- **Past performance**: relevance (size/scope/recency/duration) →
  quality → source objectivity. CPARS/PPIRS plus evaluators' personal
  knowledge; adverse info "**too close at hand**" can't be ignored
  (*Int'l Bus. Sys.*). Neutral rating for no history. Attribution of
  parents/affiliates/subs/key people allowed where they'll actually
  perform. Never downgrade for filing claims/protests/ADR (OFPP 2002).

## Clarifications vs. negotiations (the classic trap)
- **Clarifications**: resolve minor/clerical matters; no proposal
  revision; can be with one offeror only; anytime.
- **Negotiations** (discussions): any exchange giving an offeror the
  **opportunity to revise** its proposal (GAO's acid test). Once held
  with one competitive-range offeror, they must be held with all, be
  **meaningful** (identify deficiencies and significant weaknesses),
  **tailored**, equitable, and not misleading (*Metro Machine*; *SRS
  Tech* — telling an offeror its price was "too low" when support was
  the issue). No spoon-feeding required. Prohibited: favoritism,
  disclosing technical solutions or prices, revealing reference names.
- Award **without** negotiations requires the 52.215-1 notice and no
  exchanges beyond clarifications; the RFO gives COs more room for
  tailored follow-on negotiations with particular offerors (RFO FAR
  15.204-2(b)(2)).
- **Competitive range**: most highly rated proposals considering ALL
  factors including price; a range of one gets close scrutiny;
  "responsiveness" concepts don't apply.
- **Final proposal revisions**: everyone remaining gets a chance to
  revise + written notice + equal time; no duty to reopen for new
  FPR-introduced problems, but must reopen if the agency realizes it
  sat on a vital unraised issue (*Al Long Ford*).

## Award & responsibility
- Source selection decision: SSA's independent, documented comparative
  judgment; may disagree with evaluators with a reasoned basis
  (*TruLogic*); tradeoff rationale (benefits worth the price premium)
  in the SSDM. GAO reviews for reasonableness + consistency with
  criteria; contemporaneous documentation decides cases (*Apptis*).
- **Responsibility** (RFO FAR 9.103): affirmative determination
  required (financial resources, performance record, integrity,
  capability). Non-responsible small business → SBA COC referral.
  Affirmative determinations reviewable post-*Garufi* (238 F.3d 1324)
  for rational basis / ignored relevant information.

## Debriefings (the protest fuse)
- **Pre-award** (excluded offerors): request within 3 days; contents
  limited (no other offerors' info).
- **Post-award**: request in writing within **3 days** of award notice;
  held within 5 days when practicable. Must include: your deficiencies/
  significant weaknesses, both overall ratings, ranking of all
  offerors, award rationale. Never: point-by-point comparisons or
  reference identities.
- **DoD enhanced debriefings**: written/oral debrief mandatory ≥$15M;
  source selection decision document disclosed ≥$150M (optional
  request $15-150M for small/nontraditional); 2 business days for
  questions, 5 for answers, and the **CICA-stay clock runs from the
  answers** — see [Bid Protests](bid-protests.md).

## Gotchas / lessons learned
- The evaluation record is the whole game: unstated criteria, unequal
  treatment, or missing documentation lose protests that the underlying
  judgment would have won.
- One "how about revising X?" email = discussions with everyone.
- Offerors: calendar the 3-day debrief request and submit the two
  enhanced-debrief questions — they buy stay time.
