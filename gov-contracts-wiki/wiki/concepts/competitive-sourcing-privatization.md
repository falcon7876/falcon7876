# Competitive Sourcing, Insourcing & Privatization

**One-line:** Who should do the Government's work: A-76 public-private
competitions are frozen by moratorium, insourcing is fully alive (and
newly fashionable), and privatization transfers ownership — not just
performance — of housing and utilities.

*Compiled from 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook ch. 26 (whole
chapter).*

## Competitive sourcing (OMB Circular A-76)
- The framework: agencies compare Government vs. private performance
  of **commercial activities** via streamlined/standard competitions.
  A-76 (2003) remains the definitional backbone — but its competition
  machinery is **dead in practice**:
  - **Government-wide moratorium** on appropriated funds for new A-76
    competitions since 2009, renewed in every appropriations act
    (currently Pub. L. 119-75 § 741 (2026)).
  - **DoD-specific suspension** (FY10 NDAA § 325) until a
    never-published Federal Register certification issues.
  - The RFO deleted the dormant A-76 clauses from FAR Part 7.
- Practical effect: contracting out is limited to **new requirements**
  and work already performed by contractors; converting federal-
  employee work to contract performance is off the table.

## The function taxonomy (OFPP Policy Letter 11-01 — still governing)
1. **Inherently governmental** (FAIR Act definition): so intimately
   related to the public interest that federal employees must perform
   it — exercise of sovereign authority (command, prosecution,
   awarding contracts) or control of monetary transactions/
   entitlements. Contracts for these are prohibited (FAR 7.503).
   Three-step test: definition → the 24 illustrative examples →
   catch-all "nature of the function" and "exercise of discretion"
   tests.
2. **Closely associated** functions: contractible, but the agency must
   first consider reserving them for federal employees and apply
   heightened oversight (this is where [contractor personal conflicts
   of interest](ethics.md) rules attach).
3. **Critical functions**: must keep enough in-house capability to
   maintain control of the mission.
- Army implementation: the **Request for Services Contract Approval
  (RSCA)** (AR 70-13; AFARS 5107.503(e)) — the *requiring activity*
  certifies the function taxonomy before any service contract.
- Inventories: FAIR Act annual commercial/IG inventories (largely
  academic under the moratorium); the operative one is the **Inventory
  of Contracted Services** (10 U.S.C. § 4505) — services >$3M in four
  portfolio groups, contractor FTEs computed from direct labor hours.

## Insourcing (10 U.S.C. § 2463 — fully available)
- Affirmative, standing obligation to consider converting contractor
  work (and new work) to DoD civilian performance; **no competition
  required**, no caps, no quotas (unless research-based). Cost-only
  decisions must use the DTM 09-007/DoDI 7041.04 costing methodology.
- Sequencing under DoDI 7041.04: first ask inherently-governmental/
  exempt; if neither, run the Enclosure 3 cost comparison; § 2463(c)
  *prohibits* running an A-76-style competition before assigning work
  to DoD civilians.
- **2025-26 policy wave**: EO 14222 (DOGE cost-efficiency) DoD
  implementation — contractor augmentation allowed only after a
  **workforce sufficiency analysis** (not inherently governmental; no
  existing DoD capacity; gap not fixable by hiring/training/tech;
  no better rates via consolidation); IT consulting/advisory contracts
  need Deputy Secretary approval with cost-benefit analysis; SecDef
  directed termination and insourcing of various IT/consulting
  contracts (Apr. 2025). Army (May 2026) authorized senior commanders
  to bridge eliminated-civilian-position gaps with temporary contracts
  — surge relief, not conversion authority.

## Housing privatization (MHPI, 10 U.S.C. §§ 2871-85)
- Permanent authority (FY05 NDAA): convey existing housing + ~50-year
  ground lease to a private developer, who renovates/builds/manages,
  funded by servicemember rent allotments. Toolkit: direct loans,
  loan guarantees, build/lease, equity investments (with military-
  preference collateral agreements), rental guarantees, differential
  lease payments; DoD housing improvement funds.
- Agreements govern rent control, allotments, deposits, BRAC loan
  guarantees, civilian tenancy, termination. **Not subject to A-76**
  (it's an ownership transfer). Watch items (GAO has reported
  repeatedly): oversight of contractor performance, 50-year horizon
  vs. installation future, end-of-lease property decline, security
  issues with civilian residents. Davis-Bacon applies by agreement
  (see [Labor Standards](labor-standards.md)).

## Utilities privatization (10 U.S.C. § 2688)
- Service secretaries may convey utility systems (water, wastewater,
  electric, gas) to municipal/private/co-op utilities; exemptions
  (unique mission/security or uneconomical) require Service Secretary
  approval.
- **Competitive procedures required** for both the conveyance and the
  service contract; service contracts run 10 years (up to 50 with
  conveyance). Consideration may be lump-sum (credited to utility
  services/energy projects/system improvements) or future-service
  credits (limited to the base period). Congressional notice + 21-day
  wait, with a long-term economic-benefit analysis.
- **State utility monopolies don't control**: no sovereign-immunity
  waiver in § 2688, so local-franchise sole-sourcing isn't required
  (*Virginia Electric/Baltimore G&E*, GAO 2000; D. Md. agreed).
  Bundling systems into one solicitation is permissible. Protect the
  Government with **reversionary clauses** (system reverts on default
  or abandonment).

## Gotchas / lessons learned
- Don't propose an A-76 competition — both moratoria are still in
  force; the live questions are insourcing analyses and workforce
  sufficiency documentation.
- The inherently-governmental call is made by the requiring activity
  (RSCA), not the CO — get it wrong and the whole service contract is
  vulnerable.
- 2025-era approvals bite: IT consulting/advisory work now needs
  Deputy-Secretary-level signoff in DoD; check the latest memos before
  awarding any advisory services contract.
- Privatization ≠ outsourcing: ownership transfers, A-76 doesn't
  apply, and the Government's protection lives in deal terms
  (reversionary clauses, rent control, preference agreements).
