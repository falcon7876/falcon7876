# Labor Standards

**One-line:** Which labor statute governs turns on what's being bought —
construction (Davis-Bacon), services (SCA), supplies (Walsh-Healey) —
each with its own wage determinations, thresholds, and remedies, all
layered over the FLSA baseline.

*Compiled from 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook ch. 25 (whole
chapter).*

## The statute-picker
| Statute | Trigger | Threshold | Wage source | Day-to-day enforcement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FLSA** | nearly all employment | — | $7.25 federal minimum + 1.5× OT >40 hrs | DoL (+ private suits) |
| **CWHSSA** | laborers/mechanics on construction/service contracts | >$200K | OT 1.5× >40 hrs | agency (with DBA) / DoL (with SCA) |
| **Copeland** | DBA-covered construction | >$2K | anti-kickback + weekly certified payrolls | agency / DoL |
| **Davis-Bacon** | construction/alteration/repair of public works in U.S. | >$2K | DoL prevailing wage determinations | agency (DoL oversight) |
| **SCA** | contracts principally for services | >$2,500 | WDs or predecessor CBA | **DoL** |
| **Walsh-Healey** | manufacture/furnishing of supplies | >$20K | FLSA minimum (no PCA WDs in 40+ years) | DoL |
| **Defense Base Act** | contractor employees overseas | — | workers'-comp insurance mandatory pre-performance | DoL OWCP |

## Davis-Bacon (40 U.S.C. §§ 3141-48; FAR 22.4)
- Covers **laborers and mechanics** (manual/physical work, any tier;
  working foremen >20% manual) on **construction, alteration, or
  repair of public buildings/works in the U.S. >$2,000** — coverage
  set at the prime-contract level (small task orders stay covered).
  Exempt: clerical, bona fide exec/admin/professional, guards (who get
  CWHSSA overtime instead).
- **"Site of the work"** matters: primary + dedicated adjacent
  secondary sites, batch plants, tool yards; transport *within* the
  site is covered, transport *to/from* is not.
- Line-drawing: demolition covered if further construction follows;
  asbestos/paint removal usually DBA (SCA if pre-demolition);
  installation under supply contracts covered if more than incidental
  construction. **DFARS bright line** (222.402-70(d)): ≥32 work hours
  or ≥200 sq ft of painting = "repair" (DBA); less = "maintenance"
  (SCA). Non-construction contracts get DBA clauses for **substantial
  and segregable** construction work >$2K.
- **Wage determinations** (basic rate + fringes; not protestable at
  GAO or the boards — challenge inside DoL): four construction
  characters (residential/building/highway/heavy). General WDs from
  sam.gov live for the contract's life but must be refreshed at
  option exercise; project WDs (no general WD available) last 180
  days pre-award. Missing classifications get **conformed** via
  SF-1444 (pay proposed rate pending DoL's answer; true-up if higher).
- **Timing traps** (FAR 22.403): a new/revised WD ≥10 days before bid
  opening is effective (bidders must be allowed to amend); after
  opening it applies only if award slips past 90 days; awarded
  without the required WD → modify retroactively + equitable
  adjustment or terminate (*Twigg*). Negotiated: incorporate any WD
  received before award, reopen proposals. Unacknowledged WD
  amendment = nonresponsive bid (*ABC Project Mgmt.*).
- Options: update the WD at every term-extending option; price
  adjustment per whichever clause is in the contract (52.222-30
  none/-31 percentage/-32 actual).
- Remedies: suspend/withhold payments (mandatory on DoL request,
  *Westchester Fire*), T4D, debarment. **No private right of action**
  — omitted DBA clauses aren't Christian-doctrined into employee
  suits (*Universities Research v. Coutu*). MHPI/RCI privatized
  housing includes DBA by agreement.
- **Miller Act** rider: construction >$150K requires performance +
  payment bonds; unpaid subs/suppliers sue the surety in district
  court ($35K-$150K: alternative payment protections).

## Service Contract Act (41 U.S.C. §§ 6701-07; FAR 22.10)
- Contracts **principally for services** >$2,500 using service
  employees (29 C.F.R. Part 541 exempt employees excluded). Carve-outs:
  DBA construction, Walsh-Healey manufacturing (incl. true
  **remanufacturing** — complete teardown/rebuild), tariffed
  transportation, utilities, communications, direct employment, postal
  stations. Mixed workforces: SCA applies if ~20%+ of the work is by
  non-exempt employees.
- Pay = WD rate, or **predecessor CBA rates** under the successor
  contract rule (§ 4c): follow-on contracts/options for substantially
  the same services in the same locality must honor the incumbent
  CBA's wages/fringes (base period only; arm's-length and
  substantial-variance challenges go to DoL).
- WD refresh: every new solicitation, value-increasing/extending
  mods, and **annually (or biennially) on multi-year contracts**.
  CBA/WD timing: incorporate if received before award/option; not if
  received after with performance starting ≤30 days later. **The CO's
  30-day advance notice to the incumbent and union (FAR 22.1003/
  22.1010) is load-bearing** — miss it and even a late CBA binds the
  contract (*Alutiiq*, ASBCA 2020).
- **Price adjustments** (52.222-43/-44): only for DoL/Congress-driven
  increases (new WD at option, FLSA change); recovery limited to
  wages, fringes, payroll taxes, workers' comp — **no G&A, overhead,
  or profit**. No adjustment for base-year compliance with an
  existing WD (*Holmes & Narver*) or mid-term CBA raises until the
  next option (*Ameriko*). Fringe-benefit exception: unforeseen
  option-year fringe compliance costs recoverable (*Lear Siegler*).
- Nondisplacement (right-of-first-refusal) rule: **revoked** (EO
  14148, Jan. 2025) — history of flip-flops; check existing contract
  clauses before acting.
- Remedies: T4D, **3-year new-contract prohibition** (the SCA
  debarment list), withholding.

## Executive-order overlays
- **Contractor minimum wage**: EO 14026 ($15+, Biden) **revoked**
  March 2025; EO 13658 survives — **$13.30/hr** (2025) for DBA/SCA-
  covered contracts, annually adjusted, price adjustment via
  52.222-55. The EO rate prints on the first page of WDs but is
  usually below every listed rate.
- **Paid sick leave** (EO 13706; 52.222-62): 1 hour per 30 worked on
  DBA/SCA contracts; covers exempt employees too; **no price-
  adjustment mechanism**.
- **Pay transparency** (EO 13665): no retaliation for discussing
  compensation; kills confidentiality-of-pay policies on covered
  contracts.

## Labor relations on the installation
Strict Government **impartiality** in contractor labor disputes (FAR
22.104-1) — no mediating, no opining; the CO may seek continuity
assurances (52.222-1 notice-of-disputes clause). Union reps get base
access for contract-connected business, subject to security. Picketing:
**reserve-gate system** — designate one gate for the struck
contractor's people and suppliers, keep neutrals at other gates, post
signs, notify contractor and union (sample letters in ch. 25 apps.).
Route questions to the agency **contract labor advisor**.

## Gotchas / lessons learned
- The DBA/SCA boundary is decided by the DFARS 32-hour/200-sq-ft
  yardstick more often than by theory — apply it before arguing.
- Calendar the FAR 22.1003 30-day incumbent/union notice at every
  option; skipping it converts late CBA raises into Government cost.
- SCA price adjustments are NOT equitable adjustments — no profit, no
  overhead; bid option years accordingly.
- Never opine to a contractor on employee exempt status — mutual
  mistake on classification can shift the wage bill to the Government
  (*Richlin*).
- Wage determinations can't be protested at GAO or appealed to the
  boards; the only lane is DoL review — spot the issue pre-award.
