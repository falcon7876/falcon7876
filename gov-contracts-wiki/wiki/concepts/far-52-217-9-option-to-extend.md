# FAR 52.217-9 — Option to Extend the Term of the Contract

**One-line:** The clause that lets the government extend a contract into
its option years by giving written notice within a stated window.

## What it means in practice
The government may extend the contract by written notice to the
contractor within the period specified in the clause; the clause as
filled in also typically requires a preliminary notice a set number of
days before the contract expires (often 60), and caps the total duration.
The preliminary notice does **not** commit the government to exercising
the option. Reference: FAR 52.217-9 (see also FAR 17.207 for the
exercise requirements on the government side — funds available, need
continues, option is the most advantageous method).

For a contractor, the practical work is: know each contract's fill-in
values (notice days vary!), calendar the windows, and confirm pricing
and key personnel are ready before the window opens.
