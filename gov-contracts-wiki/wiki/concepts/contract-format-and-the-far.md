# Contract Format & the FAR System (incl. the RFO)

**One-line:** Every FAR contract file reads the same way — the Uniform
Contract Format's Sections A-M — and every clause traces to a FAR part
that prescribes it; since 2026 the operative text is the Revolutionary
FAR Overhaul (RFO), a statutory-roots rewrite consumed as agency class
deviations.

*Compiled from 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook chs. 2-3 (whole
chapters; the two chapters are duplicate printings of the same text —
verified by comparison, differing only in pagination and extraction
artifacts).*

## Uniform Contract Format (SF 33) — the map of any contract
| Part | Sections | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| I — The Schedule | A-H | A: solicitation/contract form · B: supplies/services & prices (**CLINs**) · C: description/specs/**SOW-PWS** · D: packaging/marking · E: inspection & acceptance · F: deliveries/performance · G: contract administration data (funding cites) · H: special contract requirements |
| II — Contract Clauses | I | the "boilerplate" — clauses required by law/regulation |
| III — Documents | J | attachments that are material parts (specs, CDRLs, DD 254) |
| IV — Reps & Instructions | K-M | K: representations & certifications · L: proposal instructions · M: evaluation factors |
- Reading discipline: **B/C/F** tell you what's being bought and
  when; **G** tells you whose money; **H** holds the acquisition's
  unique deals; **L and M must mirror each other** (instructions vs.
  evaluation — mismatches breed protests; see [Negotiated
  Procurements](negotiated-procurements.md)); Sections K-M drop out
  of the awarded contract (except incorporated reps).

## The FAR system and the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul
- The FAR (48 C.F.R. ch. 1) has governed since 1 April 1984,
  replacing the DAR/FPR/NASAPR; GSA publishes it; electronic-only
  since 2000 at acquisition.gov.
- **RFO**: under E.O. "Restoring Common Sense to Federal
  Procurement," OFPP and the FAR Council rewrote the FAR to its
  statutory roots in plain language, moving most non-statutory
  content to non-regulatory **buying guides**. Effective for DoD
  **1 Feb - 20 Apr 2026** via agency class deviations, published at
  acquisition.gov/far-overhaul. The Deskbook cites "RFO FAR"
  throughout; agency supplements (DFARS → "DRFO FARS," AFARS, etc.)
  ride as deviations; the Army's AFARS now lives on its SharePoint,
  not acquisition.gov.
- **Layout**: 8 subchapters, 53 parts (parts 20, 21, 38, 51
  reserved). Numbering: `45.602-2` = part 45, subpart 45.6, section
  45.602, subsection -2. **Clause numbers decode themselves**:
  `52.2XX-n` — the XX is the FAR part that prescribes the clause
  (52.245-2 → Government property, Part 45). The FAR Smart Matrix
  summarizes prescriptions (RFO version still pending as of May
  2026).

## When the CO must get legal review (DAFFARS 5301.602-2 list — good all-service reference)
Doubtful interpretation questions; unique/unusual provisions; actions
likely to draw public scrutiny; likely protests or claims; ADR;
liquidated damages outside construction; award-fee/award-term plans;
source selection decisions; IP/data rights/patents; bid mistakes;
protests; **ratifications**; disputes and claims; T4D/T4C (except
purchase-order cancellations); suspension/debarment; deviations; and
all J&As expected to exceed the FAR 6.304 threshold ($900K under the
RFO). Army practice (AFARS 5101.602-2-90): local procedures govern,
but counsel participates through the whole acquisition.

## The contract-review checklist (Attachment 1 — the working tool)
A solicitation/award review walks:
1. **Purchase request & funds** — PR in file, delivery dates and
   descriptions consistent, proper fund cite, purpose/time/amount
   checks, 10 U.S.C. § 2410a severable-services crossing authority,
   availability-of-funds clauses where needed
   ([Funding & Fund Limitations](funding-and-fund-limitations.md)).
2. **Method & competition** — sealed bidding required? exclusions and
   J&As proper and approved? SAT thresholds ($350K; contingency
   $1M/$2M) invoked correctly? required sources (Part 8) checked?
   ([Competition](competition.md), [Simplified
   Acquisitions](simplified-acquisitions.md)).
3. **Publicizing** — synopsis, adequate response time, combined
   synopsis/solicitation for commercial buys.
4. **Solicitation mechanics** — dates/times consistent, required
   clauses, Part 36 rules for construction.
5. **Evaluation factors** — stated, clear, not unduly restrictive,
   relative importance disclosed, past performance included.
6. **Pricing & contract type** — pricing method clear, audit/TINA
   clauses, type appropriate, personal-services determination,
   requirements/IDIQ quantity estimates reasonable and clauses in,
   multiple-award preference addressed ([Contract
   Types](contract-types.md), [Contract Pricing](contract-pricing.md)).
7. **Specs, QA, delivery** — unambiguous purchase descriptions,
   brand-name-or-equal used properly, inspection/acceptance points
   named, delivery schedule realistic
   ([Inspection, Acceptance & Warranty](inspection-acceptance-warranty.md)).
8. **Bonds/LDs, GFP, small business, environmental, labor** — Part 28
   bonds, LD rates reasonable, GFP identified with responsibilities
   allocated, set-aside/8(a) eligibility and NAICS code, wage
   determinations in ([Socioeconomic
   Policies](socioeconomic-policies.md), [Labor
   Standards](labor-standards.md)).
9. **Clarity** — read the whole thing; hunt ambiguities and
   inconsistencies.
10. **Award review** — amendments distributed, late/mistaken bids
    handled, responsiveness and responsibility determined (COC
    referral if small business), evaluation followed Section M,
    BCMs complete, TINA cert signed, prices in the contract, funds
    sufficient ([Sealed Bidding](sealed-bidding.md),
    [Responsibility, Timeliness & OCI](responsibility-timeliness-oci.md)).

The chapters attach a full **sample solicitation** (Pentagon custodial
services PWS with performance matrix, past-performance questionnaire,
and DD 254) — a worked example of every UCF section, useful as a
model when drafting Section C/L/M language.

## Gotchas / lessons learned
- Chapters 2 and 3 of the 2026 edition are the same chapter printed
  twice — cite either; there is no substantive "variant B."
- The RFO transition is live: clause numbers and thresholds cited in
  older files (and in DAFFARS, per the Deskbook's own note on the
  $750K→$900K J&A threshold) may not match the RFO text — verify
  against acquisition.gov/far-overhaul, and note the RFO Smart
  Matrix was still pending as of May 2026.
- L/M consistency and Section B/C/F coherence are where most
  reviewable defects hide; the checklist's last item ("read the
  entire solicitation") is the one that actually catches them.
- Clause numbers self-decode (52.2**XX** → prescribing part) — the
  fastest way to find the rule governing any clause you don't
  recognize.
