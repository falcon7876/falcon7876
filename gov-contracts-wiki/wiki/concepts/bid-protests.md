# Bid Protests (Agency & GAO)

**One-line:** How disappointed offerors challenge solicitations and
awards — at the agency, at GAO under CICA, or at COFC — with the
timeliness traps and the automatic-stay mechanics that decide most
protests in practice.

*Compiled from 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook ch. 15A (whole
chapter). COFC litigation is ch. 15B (not yet compiled).*

## Framework
- **Three forums**: agency (E.O. 12979; FAR 33.103 / RFO FAR 33.104),
  GAO (CICA, 31 U.S.C. §§ 3551-3556; 4 C.F.R. pt. 21), COFC (Tucker Act,
  28 U.S.C. § 1491(b)). Agency protest first is encouraged, never
  required (FAR 33.102(e)).
- **Who may protest**: an "interested party" — actual/prospective
  offeror whose direct economic interest is affected. Pre-bid-opening:
  anyone who expressed interest in competing. Post-submission: actual
  offerors, generally "next in line" for award (*Ogden Support Servs.*,
  B-270354.2) or those who'd regain a chance to compete via
  resolicitation (*Remtech*, B-240402). On multiple-award vehicles, one
  awardee can't protest another's award (*Aegis Defense Servs.*,
  B-412755).
- **If the answer would still be "no award to you," no standing**
  (*DGCI Corp.*, B-418494).

## Timeliness (4 C.F.R. § 21.2) — where protests die
| Situation | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Defect apparent on the face of the solicitation | **Before** bid opening / proposal due date |
| Basis arises from an amendment | Next due date for revised proposals |
| After a **required** debriefing (competitive proposals, incl. task orders) | 10 days after the debriefing is held (but file **within 5 days** to get the CICA stay) |
| Everything else | 10 days after protester knew or should have known the basis |
| GAO after an agency-level protest | 10 days after actual/constructive knowledge of initial adverse agency action — pursuing an agency appeal does NOT toll it (*Telestar*, B-247029) |
| Reconsideration at GAO | 10 days; new facts required |

- Days are calendar days; filing is through **EPDS** ($500 fee).
  Protester must give the agency a full copy within 1 day.
- **Premature protests** are also dismissed: can't file before the
  offered debriefing (*Kord Techs.*, B-417748.5). A debrief that was
  offered but not *required* doesn't extend anything (*Centerra*,
  B-418628).
- **DoD enhanced debriefings**: 2 business days to submit questions,
  agency answers within 5; the debrief isn't "concluded" until answers
  arrive — but skipping questions forfeits the extra days (*NIKA Tech.
  v. U.S.*, 987 F.3d 1024 (Fed. Cir. 2021)).
- Protesters must show **due diligence** (request the debrief, attend
  bid opening, use GAO document production rather than slow FOIA).
- Escape hatches (rarely granted): significant-issue and good-cause
  exceptions (4 C.F.R. § 21.2(c)).

## The CICA stay (31 U.S.C. § 3553) — the real leverage
- **Pre-award**: GAO notice of a timely protest freezes award.
- **Post-award**: performance suspends if the protest is filed within
  **10 days of award** or **5 days after the offered required-debriefing
  date**, whichever is later (DoD adds 10 days after task/delivery
  orders >$25M).
- The stay triggers on **GAO's notice to the agency**, not the filing —
  though EPDS makes that near-instant.
- **Override**: HCA-level, nondelegable, written D&F — pre-award needs
  "urgent and compelling"; post-award "best interests" or urgent and
  compelling. Army routes overrides through DASA(P)/AMC HCA (AFARS
  5133.104). GAO won't review an override (*Banknote*, B-245528), but
  **COFC will** (*Cigna*, 70 Fed. Cl. 100 — arbitrary and capricious;
  *URS Fed. Servs.*, 102 Fed. Cl. 664 — four-factor test).
- Funds don't expire until 100 days after protest resolution (31 U.S.C.
  § 1558) — matters for the Aug-Nov protest season.

## GAO jurisdiction highlights
- Covers federal procurements of property/services, including "reverse
  protests" of terminations premised on award improprieties, agency use
  of an OT agreement when a procurement contract is required (*Oracle
  America*, B-416061), and leases where the agency effectively procures
  services (*Blue Origin*, B-408823).
- Does **not** cover: contract administration; SBA size/status/COC/8(a)
  matters (narrow exceptions); affirmative responsibility determinations
  (exceptions: definitive responsibility criteria, fraud/bad faith,
  ignored information); subcontractor protests; suspension/debarment;
  matters in federal court; FAA/TSA (AMS-exempt); task/delivery orders
  except scope-increase or >$10M civilian / >$35M DoD (order value
  includes priced 52.217-8 options, *Adams & Assocs.*, B-417534).

## Merits review
- APA-like reasonableness review, no de novo; protester bears the
  burden (*EA Engineering*, B-411967.2) and must also show **prejudice**
  — a substantial chance of award but for the error (*Odyssey Mktg.*,
  B-412695); doubts on prejudice go to the protester (*KBR-Recon*,
  B-309752.8).
- Agencies win on documentation: inadequate contemporaneous records risk
  GAO finding the evaluation unsupported (*Harmonia*, B-417475.3);
  litigation-driven reevaluations get little weight (*Boeing Sikorsky*,
  B-277263.2).
- Bad-faith allegations need convincing proof; good faith is presumed.
- Unduly-restrictive-specs protests shift burdens: agency justifies the
  restriction, then protester must show it clearly unreasonable
  (*J. Squared*, B-408388).

## Remedies & costs
- GAO issues **recommendations** (recompete, terminate, new
  solicitation, don't exercise options...); not binding, but refusals
  are reported to Congress and agencies almost always comply
  (*Honeywell*, 870 F.2d 644).
- Decision due in **100 days** (65 under the express option). GAO ADR:
  negotiation assistance, litigation risk assessment, outcome
  prediction.
- Protest costs + attorneys' fees (capped $150/hr for other than small
  businesses) recommended when protest is clearly meritorious and agency
  dragged its feet; prompt corrective action (by agency-report due date)
  defeats fees (*Kertzman*, B-259461). Claim entitlement within 15 days
  of corrective-action closure; quantum claim within 60 days of
  decision. No lost/anticipatory profits (*Keco Indus.*, 192 Ct. Cl.
  773).

## Agency-protest specifics worth remembering
- Solicitation defects: file before opening/closing; everything else 10
  days. "Filed" = received by 4:30 p.m. local.
- Agency pre-award stay is regulatory; post-award stay requires filing
  within 10 days of award or 5 of a required debrief. Overrides need
  written approval above the CO.
- Agency must use best efforts to decide within **35 days**; adverse
  action there starts the 10-day GAO clock immediately.

## Gotchas / lessons learned
- The 5-day (stay) vs. 10-day (timeliness) debriefing windows are
  different clocks — calendar both.
- An agency-level protest does not pause the GAO clock. Ever.
- On the agency side: document the evaluation contemporaneously; the
  record you didn't write is the protest you lose.
