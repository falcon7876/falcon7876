# 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook (TJAGLCS)

**One-line:** The Army JAG School's annual contract & fiscal law
reference — 37 chapters covering the full lifecycle of federal
acquisition, from formation through disputes and special topics.

## Bibliographic info
- **Publisher:** Contract & Fiscal Law Department, The Judge Advocate
  General's Legal Center and School (TJAGLCS), U.S. Army, Charlottesville, VA
- **Edition:** 2026 · 1,651 pages
- **Public source:** [tjaglcs.army.mil publications page](https://tjaglcs.army.mil/Publications)
  (U.S. government work — no copyright restriction)
- **Local files:** `raw/2026-acquisition-attorneys-deskbook.pdf` (17.5 MB)
  and per-chapter PDFs in `raw/deskbook-chapters/` produced by
  `scripts/split_deskbook.py` (all gitignored — re-download and re-split
  if missing)

## ⚠️ Edition quirks (verified against the PDF, 2026-07-07)
- The printed **Summary of Contents is stale** — trust the chapter map
  below (built from actual in-book headings), not the book's own SOC.
  E.g., the SOC lists "Contract Law Research" (ch. 2) which does not
  exist in the body, and places Contract Changes at 17 when it is
  actually chapter 18.
- **Chapters 2 and 3 are near-duplicate versions** of "Contract Format
  and the FAR" — the 2026 edition appears caught mid-transition to the
  FAR Overhaul (it cites "RFO FAR" provisions throughout). Compare both
  before relying on either.
- No plain chapter 15 or 19; protests are 15A/15B and disputes are
  19A/19B.

## Notes for the agent
- Compile lazily per the reference-work rules in `CLAUDE.md`: read a
  chapter (from its split PDF) when a question or contract touches it,
  then write/enrich `concepts/` and `cases/` pages citing "2026
  Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook, ch. N" with chapter-relative page
  numbers (e.g., "ch. 18 at 18-5").
- The book cites cases constantly; create `cases/` pages only for
  decisions load-bearing enough to come up again, and list the rest
  inline in concept pages.
- Chapter 2/3 include a **sample solicitation/contract award review
  checklist** attachment — worth its own concept page early.
