# 2026 Acquisition Attorneys Deskbook (TJAGLCS)

**One-line:** The Army JAG School's annual contract & fiscal law
reference — 35 chapters across 3 volumes covering the full lifecycle of
federal acquisition, from formation through disputes and special topics.

## Bibliographic info
- **Publisher:** Contract & Fiscal Law Department, The Judge Advocate
  General's Legal Center and School (TJAGLCS), U.S. Army, Charlottesville, VA
- **Edition:** 2026
- **Public source:** [tjaglcs.army.mil publications page](https://tjaglcs.army.mil/Publications)
  (U.S. government work — no copyright restriction)
- **Local file:** `raw/2026-acquisition-attorneys-deskbook.pdf` (17.5 MB,
  ~1,600 pages; gitignored — download from the public source above if missing)
